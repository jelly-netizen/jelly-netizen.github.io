Web Browsers

Overview

A web browser is the application that fetches content from the web and displays it on a user's device. This site looks at the basic architecture and design of web browsers, how browsers started and what they looked like in the beginning, and how they have changed over the years. It gives an overview of today's most popular browsers, follows the rise and fall of certain browsers during the browser wars, and looks ahead to what browsers could become.
